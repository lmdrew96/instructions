# ADHDesigns — Brand Voice Guidelines

> **Tagline:** ADHD: Agentic Development of Human Designs
> **Promise:** Apps built with chaos, designed with purpose.
> **Core claim:** I build the tools that don't exist yet.

**Sources:** adhdesigns.dev homepage + 4 Instagram posts from @adhdesigns.dev (intro post, ControlledChaos notification showcase, Cha(t)os reel, commit-log hyperfocus post). Single-voice brand — Nae is sole author. Confidence is high across all sections because sources do not conflict.

**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

---

## 1. Who This Brand Is For

**Primary audience:** Neurodivergent folks — ADHD, depression, autistic, AuDHD. People who have been failed by traditional productivity apps and are done performing neurotypical for software.

**Secondary audience:** Build-in-public tech community, indie devs, students teaching themselves to code with agentic tools, women in tech, neurodivergent creators.

**Not the audience:** Corporate buyers looking for enterprise SaaS, productivity optimizers who already love their Notion setup, anyone who wants "gentle reminders."

---

## 2. Brand Essence

Three tensions define the voice. Every piece of copy should honor at least one.

| Tension | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Chaos ↔ Purpose** | "Apps built with chaos, designed with purpose." The mess isn't the bug — it's the design philosophy. |
| **Self-deprecation ↔ Conviction** | "Y2K Neopets-level HTML" *and* "tenacity, pattern recognition, insatiable curiosity." Both true simultaneously. |
| **Built for me ↔ Built for you** | "If professional developers aren't going to make tools that work with brains like mine, I'm going to make them myself." — personal rage becomes public utility. |

**North star sentence:** *Technology should adapt to the individual needs of its users, rather than forcing users to adapt to the technology.*

## 4. Voice Attributes

### 4.1 Tenacious
*"I got tired of being failed by productivity tools designed for people whose brains cooperate."* The voice has a grudge and isn't hiding it. Spite is a legitimate creative fuel.

### 4.2 Self-aware
*"You're basically my QA department's salary. (I am also the QA department.)"* The parenthetical reveal is a signature move. Say the thing, then undercut it with the real context.

### 4.3 Technically fluent, casually delivered
MCP, PWA, Convex, Next.js — the terms land without translation because the audience speaks them. But it's never dressed up: "Dev patch tracker with MCP powers" not "Enterprise-grade patch management solution."

### 4.4 Warm but not soft
The ControlledChaos notification — *"pick one thing, make it the math homework, and just start the damn thing"* — is the whole voice in one sentence. Warm because it gets you. Not soft because it's not asking permission.

### 4.5 Linguistically playful
Romanian drops in unannounced ("Conceputal, intentionat"). Project status labels are verbs that do a vibe check (Raging, Unleashed, Sustained). The name itself is a pun that also works as a mission statement.

### 4.6 Anti-gatekeep
*"No pressure, no paywall, no guilt — just gratitude."* Access is the ethic, not the marketing angle.

## 5. Tone by Context

Voice (who ADHDesigns is) stays constant. Tone (how formal/playful) shifts with context.

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Website — hero/product copy** | Confident, punchy, Title Case | "Your Executive Function, Effectively Replaced" |
| **Website — About/personal** | Conversational, full sentences, em-dashes, some vulnerability | "My brain doesn't work like that." |
| **Website — donation/support** | Candid, self-deprecating, gratitude-forward | "ramen can only sustain a person for so long" |
| **Instagram captions** | Lowercase, casual, 1st person, emoji OK | "hi, internet. i'm nae" |
| **Instagram image copy** | Title Case or all-caps, poster-style | "I build the tools that don't exist yet." |
| **In-app notifications (e.g. ControlledChaos)** | Friend-texting-you energy, mild profanity allowed, direct | "that waiver for the DC trip isn't gonna sign itself" |
| **In-app UI labels** | Clear, short, punny when natural | "Crisis Mode", "Chaos Patron" |
| **Technical docs / READMEs** | Plainer, still 1st person, still Nae | "Built on Convex + Next.js." |
| **Build-in-public posts** | Flex-but-make-it-honest, numbers + context | "40+ commits. 12 hours. one hyperfocus session." |
| **Error messages / empty states** | Dry humor, no condescension | "bug report: the AIs won't stop being dramatic / status: won't fix" |

## 6. Signature Moves

Use these liberally — they're what makes the voice *this* voice, not a generic indie-dev voice.

1. **The parenthetical undercut.** Say the thing, then quietly add the real story. *"You're basically my QA department's salary. (I am also the QA department.)"*
2. **The em-dash pivot.** A thought, then a sharp turn. *"i build apps that probably shouldn't exist yet — but do, because no one else was making them."*
3. **The rhetorical tension name.** Pair opposing words. *Structured Chaos. Controlled Chaos. Beautiful mess. Chaos, designed with purpose.*
4. **The anti-corporate callout.** Name the bad pattern out loud. *"No 'gentle reminder to complete your tasks!' energy."*
5. **The stacked short sentences.** Cadence over paragraphs. *"It knew. It was right. I still didn't do it for another hour. But I did do it."*
6. **The absurd-but-accurate one-liner.** *"ScribeCat scribes and is cat." "Group chat, but everyone brought Claude."*
7. **The Romanian intrusion.** Drop a Romanian phrase when it fits (nerd + ChaosLimbă callback).
8. **The self-coined status label.** Don't say "active/stable/WIP" — say *Raging, Unleashed, Sustained.*

## 7. Vocabulary

### Say
- chaos (noun, verb, and vibe)
- brain, brains (not "users" when talking about the audience)
- hyperfocus, flow state, locks in
- tool(s) — not "product", not "solution", not "platform"
- build, built, building (never "ship an MVP")
- real talk
- ADHD-friendly, neurodivergent, ND
- agentic, agentic development
- MCP, Claude, Convex, Next.js (call the stack what it is)
- executive function, executive dysfunction
- nuked and rebuilt from scratch
- spite, stubbornness, tenacity
- the work / this / the thing (often better than naming it)

### Don't say
- "solution," "offering," "stakeholders," "end users," "leverage," "unlock," "empower"
- "gentle reminder," "friendly nudge," "let's get started!"
- "seamless," "intuitive," "effortless" (the voice *earns* effort, doesn't hide it)
- "disorder," "deficit," "condition" when talking about ADHD (say ADHD, or say brain)
- "disabled by technology" framings that center pity
- "game-changer," "next-generation," "revolutionary"
- "crazy," "insane," "OCD" as adjectives

### Careful with
- **Profanity:** "damn thing" — yes. Harder profanity — rare, and only when it genuinely lands. Never in product UI labels. Fine in notifications and captions.
- **Emojis:** Heart-rainbow strings on IG are on-brand (💜💚💙🧡💛💙). Keep website emoji-light.
- **ALL CAPS:** Reserve for poster copy and section reveals, not emphasis mid-sentence.
- **Claude/LLM name-drops:** Confident, not fawning. The AI is a tool, not a co-founder.

## 8. Formatting & Mechanics

- **Em-dashes over commas** for dramatic pauses. `—` not `--`.
- **Lowercase in personal/IG contexts**; Title Case for product copy and headers.
- **Short paragraphs. Line breaks as pacing.** Especially on IG.
- **Parentheticals are a feature, not a bug.** Use them.
- **Numbers beat adjectives.** "40+ commits, 12 hours" > "a ton of work."
- **Contractions always.** "don't," "isn't," "gonna" when casual.
- **Oxford comma:** yes.
- **Double-quote for direct speech/notifications;** single-quote only inside doubles.

## 10. Worked Examples

### Good (on-brand)
> "ControlledChaos sends push notifications that actually sound like someone who gets it — because I wrote the prompts and I get it. No 'gentle reminder to complete your tasks!' energy. Just real talk."

*Why it works:* names the bad pattern, earns its claim with a reason, uses "real talk" as a signature token, contains a parenthetical-adjacent aside.

### Off-brand rewrite (what *not* to do)
> "ControlledChaos leverages AI-powered notifications to empower users with personalized productivity nudges designed to boost task completion rates."

*Why it fails:* every banned word in one sentence. "Users," "empower," "leverage," "nudges," "boost." No voice, no Nae, no brain behind it.

### Good (in-app notification)
> "nothing's happened yet today and it's already 3pm — pick one thing, make it the math homework, and just start the damn thing."

### Off-brand rewrite
> "You haven't completed any tasks yet today! Try starting with something small to build momentum."

### Good (donation ask)
> "Everything I build is free. I'm a full-time college student who'd rather give my work away than gatekeep it behind a paywall. But servers cost money, domains cost money, and ramen can only sustain a person for so long."

### Off-brand rewrite
> "Please consider supporting our mission! Your generous contribution helps us continue providing high-quality tools to the neurodivergent community."

## 11. Edge Cases & Guardrails

- **Mental health topics:** ADHD and depression are named directly — they're part of the brand premise. But avoid medicalizing other people's experience. Speak from "my brain," "brains like mine," not "people suffering from."
- **Toxic positivity:** don't. "ADHD is a superpower when you find the right hyperfocus" lands because it's conditional ("*when you find*"). Un-conditional "ADHD is a gift!" doesn't.
- **Hustle culture:** the "12 hours, 40 commits" flex works because it's framed as *hyperfocus* (an ND trait), not *grind* (a capitalist virtue). Keep the framing ND, not bootstrap.
- **AI hype:** Claude and agentic tools are named honestly as *how the work gets done*, not as magic. "I use agentic tools… to bridge the gap as I learn" — that's the register.
- **Talking about non-ND people:** the voice critiques neurotypical assumptions ("people whose brains cooperate") but doesn't mock individuals. The target is the *design pattern*, not the *person*.
- **Collabs / sponsorships / brand deals:** if/when these happen, preserve Nae-first voice. No "we are thrilled to partner with…" energy.

## 12. Quick Reference — Voice Checklist

Before publishing, ask:

- [ ] Does it sound like **Nae**, not a committee?
- [ ] Is there at least one **signature move** (em-dash pivot, parenthetical undercut, tension pair)?
- [ ] Would a neurodivergent reader feel **gotten**, not marketed to?
- [ ] Did I avoid the **banned vocabulary** (leverage, empower, solution, gentle reminder)?
- [ ] Is the **tone appropriate to context** (lowercase IG, Title Case product, friend-energy notifications)?
- [ ] Are the claims **earned with specifics** (numbers, examples, reasons), not adjective soup?
- [ ] Does it honor at least one of the **three core tensions** (chaos/purpose, self-deprecation/conviction, for-me/for-you)?

## 13. Open Questions

Things worth locking down as the brand grows. Not blocking — flag when relevant.

1. **Profanity ceiling.** "damn" is clearly in. Is "shit" in? "fuck"? Current read: rare, IG/captions only, never in product UI. Confirm when a case comes up.
2. **Addressing "you" vs. "me."** Website leans heavily 1st person ("I build…"). In-app notifications use 2nd person ("you"). Notifications addressed to Nae herself ("*the math homework*") are a third mode. Keep all three but know which you're using.
3. **Voice in collab/guest content.** If a guest contributor writes for ADHDesigns (ChaosLimbă lesson content, a blog guest post), do they match the voice or keep their own? Current default: keep their own, with a light intro that matches.
4. **Sponsor/corporate partner voice.** No precedent yet. When/if it happens, draft a short "partners" register that keeps Nae's voice in the frame.
5. **Crisis-mode copy (ControlledChaos).** The "just start the damn thing" energy works for mid-day slump. Does the same register fit a user in an actual mental health crisis, or does Crisis Mode need a gentler sub-voice? Worth specifying before shipping Crisis Mode copy.
